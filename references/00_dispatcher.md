## When to Use
- You are deploying programmatic SEO rollouts or acquiring expired domains.
- You are linting a bulk location-page rollout: the pre-deploy gate blocks folders whose pages fail the Swap Test after locality masking (>50% shared substance), duplicate/unlinked JSON-LD, or first-person claims on reused assets (Module 19; rule definitions are `../../kirby-local-seo/SKILL.md` Module 7).
- You need to review publishing cadences or network footprint isolation.
- You are executing the Minimum Viable Page (MVP) content testing protocol.
- You are launching third-party satellite sites or EMDs for firewalled experiments.
- You are establishing deployment guardrails to prevent runaway AI publishing penalties.
- You are staging hub-page keywords to accumulate pre-ranking relevance before launching child conversion pages (Module 7 §7.16).
- You are deciding between HTTP 410 and 404 for retired URLs (§7.7 — 410 drops dead URLs ~2x faster; passive 404s left 49.6% indexed per the §7.7 benchmark).
- You are auditing multilingual/English mirrors for parametric-poisoning staleness (§7.17 — already-ingested poisoning may be permanent; synchronize, consolidate, or deliberately retire).
- You are evaluating "satellite hub directory" plays (Module 17 §17.4 — PARKED, no verifiable source; the Module 17 third-party EMD firewall is the sanctioned satellite doctrine).
- You are deploying defensive video assets to mitigate negative branded AI Overviews (Module 18).
- You are staging a site migration or EMD/satellite cutover against Google's empirical consolidation windows — 1–3 months typical, 6 months-to-1+-year slow case, canonical convergence 1–3 weeks — with the pre-cutover signal purge, single-event flip, sitemap discipline, 90-day freeze, and month-6 escalation gate (Module 20; benchmark table and forecast math are `../../kirby-seo-timing-benchmarks/SKILL.md`).
- You are setting post-deploy expectations for title/meta/snippet edits — 1–2 day SERP refresh typical *after* the ~30-day recrawl gate, so verification windows run 4–6 weeks, not next-day (Module 20 §20.3).
- You are staging a domain migration or rebrand attached to a Google Business Profile: enforce the mandatory 14-day indexing buffer post-cutover (T+14d) before touching GBP, then stagger GBP updates at 1 sensitive field per week (URL -> Name -> Category/Phone) from the Primary Owner account (Module 20 §20.4; full GBP rebranding and verification rules live in `../../kirby-local-seo/SKILL.md` Module 9 & Module 12).
