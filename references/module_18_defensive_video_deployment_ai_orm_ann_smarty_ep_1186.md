# Module 18: Defensive Video Deployment for AI ORM

*Source: Ann Smarty & Edward Sturm podcast Episode 1,186. October 3, 2026.*

### **18.1 The Threat to Branded SERPs**
Google's AI Overviews heavily cite video content when forming summaries for branded queries. If the SERP for your brand contains video reviews highlighting a "Scam" or questioning if a brand is "Legit," the AI Overview will proactively summarize those negative points directly at the top of the search results page.

### **18.2 Targeted Video Deployment**
To defend against this, companies must aggressively deploy positive, controlled video assets targeting hostile search intents.
*   **Targeting High-Risk Modifiers:** Produce dedicated video testimonials, reviews, and detailed product walk-throughs titled explicitly to intercept high-risk modifiers:
    *   `[Brand] Review - Honest Feedback`
    *   `Is [Brand] a Scam? The Truth`
    *   `[Brand] Legit? Real Customer Experience`
*   **The Deployment Hub:** Host these defensive videos on major platforms (primarily YouTube, due to Google's preferential crawling and indexing). 
*   **Optimizing for LLM Ingestion:** Provide highly detailed, structured text descriptions and accurate closed captions (CC). LLMs ingest the text transcripts of these videos to form their AI Overviews. By owning the top video results for these specific intents, the brand forces the AI to ingest and display a positive, controlled narrative instead of a third-party hit piece.
